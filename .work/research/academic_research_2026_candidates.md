## 1. Academic Paradigm Shifts (2025–2026)

Research from ACM/IEEE CS2023, SIGCSE, and top-tier university curricula indicates 4 major paradigm shifts:
1. **Competency & AI Artifact Verification over Rote Boilerplate Coding:** With GenAI automating basic syntax writing, academic curricula are pivoting to teaching students system verification, code auditing, and evaluating AI-generated artifacts.
2. **Sustainable Computing & Green AI:** Driven by the carbon footprint and compute costs of large models, Stanford and MIT have embedded energy-aware computing and model efficiency into core CS syllabi.
3. **Integrated AI Governance & Red-Teaming:** AI Ethics has evolved from isolated humanities electives into integrated technical practices (Red-Teaming, Guardrails, AI Governance Lifecycle).
4. **Probabilistic vs. Deterministic Reasoning:** A fundamental shift from teaching purely rule-based deterministic logic to data-driven probabilistic reasoning and Explainable AI (XAI).
