# Literature Survey: Apple AR Ecosystem & Pedagogy

- **Relevance:** Establishes the authoritative architectural standard for Apple 3D/AR apps.

## 3. XR & Apple Spatial Developer Learning Frameworks

- **Key Concepts:** Progressive disclosure from 2D SwiftUI -> 3D SIMD math -> AR World Tracking -> Advanced Occlusion/Face tracking.
